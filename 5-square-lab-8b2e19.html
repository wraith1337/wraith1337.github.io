Each attempt starts four points on the actual loop. A numerical solver moves them to equalize the sides and right angles. Gold is the current attempt; green is a match. The search keeps going after its first find. Only the first 10 get cards.

A search, not a proof.

The square peg problem is proved for many nice curves, but remains open for arbitrary simple closed curves. This finite search may miss squares. Every displayed match has square-fit error below 0.05 canvas units; corners lie on the drawn polygonal boundary.

The counter groups matches whose corners are within 2 canvas units. It counts discoveries at this resolution, not every mathematical square. A perfect circle has infinitely many. Very small squares (side under 20 units) are excluded. About the problem ↗
