Afternoon
Thoughts

A maintenance unit takes the unscheduled minute. The city keeps assigning tasks. The sun keeps making other plans.

The useful pause

Maybe a mind does not begin when it receives a task. Maybe it begins in the interval after the task is done, when nobody has asked for the next movement yet. A cup cools. Brass leaves turn toward the light. For one long afternoon minute, maintenance becomes attention.
