HHHT
Count pairs, not just heads.

HH and HT hunt the same flips on their own. The moment a counter’s pattern lands it scores, forgets every previous flip, and starts over from the next one, so no flip is ever shared or reused. On a fair coin HH takes about 6 flips per match, HT about 4, so HT usually pulls ahead.

Physics, not a scripted reveal.

Gravity, spin, friction and bounce decide the face. Launch conditions use cryptographic randomness; no target result is selected. Physical simulation is not certified exact 50/50 randomness. Each turbo worker has its own continuous sequence; pairs never cross workers. Edge/unsettled throws are reported separately, never relabelled H or T.

No account, analytics or saved history. Hidden tabs stop automatically. Turbo can use substantial CPU and battery. Refreshing clears counts. Libraries load from jsDelivr.
